Month 1 Flood Analysis Summary

Question

Does proximity to waterbodies indicate areas of potential flood exposure in Kaduna North LGA?

Spatial Operation

I ran a 500 m buffer around waterbodies using the projected CRS EPSG:32632. I chose buffering because it identifies areas within a specified distance of waterbodies and can help assess potential flood exposure.

Expected and Actual Result

I expected approximately 38 buffer features, one for each waterbody. I got 38 buffer features, and all 38 were valid.

What Surprised Me

The number of buffer features matched my expectation exactly.

Data Still Needed

I still need actual flood hazard or flood occurrence data, as well as elevation and possibly building or population data, to identify areas that are truly exposed to flooding rather than only areas close to waterbodies.
